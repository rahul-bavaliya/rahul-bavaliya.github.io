A chatbot that estimates how long a service job will take. It blends retrieval over historical work orders with a gradient-boosted model, so service advisors get a number and the past jobs that justify it.

  • NVIDIA NIM embeddings + Llama 3.1-70B for synthesis, ChromaDB vector store
